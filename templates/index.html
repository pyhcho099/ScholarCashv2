Roles & Permissions

A structured hierarchy ensures control, accountability, and transparency.

👑
Administrator

Principal

Controls total supply, manages branches, classes, staff, store items, and oversees all transactions.

🎓
Manager

Dept. Head

Receives budget from Principal, distributes coins to teachers, and monitors branch statistics.

📝
Distributor

Teacher / Tutor

Rewards students with coins for performance. Tutors can also add students to their classes.

🎒
User

Student

Earns coins, views history, browses the school store, and redeems rewards with unique codes.
